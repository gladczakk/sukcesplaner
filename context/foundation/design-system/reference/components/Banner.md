# Banner

A one-line warning above content, used for the guardrail: an approaching milestone deadline is signaled before it passes.

`sp-banner` (ochre, milestone soon) or `sp-banner--late` (brick, something is overdue), with `role="status"`, an icon and one sentence that names the milestone in bold. Desktop: top of the rail. Mobile: under the date heading, one banner at most (the most urgent).
