# Navigation

Sidebar on desktop, bottom tab bar on phones; the same three destinations: Dziś, Kalendarz, Projekty.

- Sidebar `sp-nav` on `river`, 240px wide: brand, the three items (`sp-nav__item`, `aria-current="page"` on the active one, a count on the right), then the project list with dots.
- Tab bar `sp-tabbar` under 768px, with the add button `sp-fab` floating above it on the right.
- The right rail (milestones) appears from 1100px; below that its most urgent item becomes a banner.
